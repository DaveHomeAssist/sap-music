05 — Voice
Brand Voice & Tone
Low-Key Cool
Confident, not bragging. The music speaks. Copy supports but doesn't oversell. No hype for hype's sake.
Technical
Monospace aesthetic. Hardware references. Precision-minded. Labels read like instrument panels, not marketing copy.
Warm
Genuine appreciation for the listener. "If you're here reading this, thank you." Not cold — just focused.
Direct
No fluff, no placeholder copy. Only real info — real play counts, real show dates, real contacts.
Bass-First
The identity leans into the low end — heavy, physical, sub-bass culture. Not pretty, not pop. Textured.
Global
South Florida origin, international scope. 140+ shows, 12 countries. The brand travels. Keep it universal.
