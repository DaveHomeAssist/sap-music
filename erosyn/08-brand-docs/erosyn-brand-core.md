# Erosyn identity and voice

## Artist

Erosyn is Connor's electronic music project, based in Miami and previously known as SAP. The music and DJ sets move through bass music and rave styles.

## Core line

Finding beauty in the dark.

## Website voice

Write like a person talking about music. Use first person in the artist introduction and third person in a press biography. Keep the humor and informal details in track descriptions. Avoid claims about career size, touring, equipment or availability unless confirmed.

Give each section a job: help someone listen, learn about Connor, find a show, download press materials or get in touch. Do not repeat a list of genres or describe the visual design in every paragraph.

## Reusable copy

Use the current biography, release links and contact details in [press-bio.md](../02-press-kit/press-bio.md). Its source is [press-copy.json](../02-press-kit/press-copy.json).

## Visual direction

Keep the black, red and bone palette, clear type and physical studio references. The visuals can be dark and textured while buttons and instructions stay plain. Use the flat logo when readability matters. Keep SAP to brief historical context.
