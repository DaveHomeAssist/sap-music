Erosyn is the rebranded producer/DJ project from Connor, formerly SAP. Based in Miami with New Jersey roots, Erosyn pushes experimental bass, dubstep, DnB, UK grime, psytrance, electro, hardstyle, and classic rave into a darker hybrid sound built for underground rooms, SoundCloud heads, and late-night rave systems.

## 📚 Medium Bio

Erosyn is a Miami-based producer and DJ project from Connor, formerly known as SAP. The sound stays rooted in experimental bass, dubstep, DnB, UK grime, psytrance, electro, hardstyle, and classic rave, but the new identity sharpens the project into something darker, more cinematic, and more physically intense.

Built around distorted low-end, unstable rhythm, cyber-rave textures, and warehouse tension, Erosyn is not chasing polished festival EDM. It is a mutation of bass music, rave culture, and underground club pressure.
