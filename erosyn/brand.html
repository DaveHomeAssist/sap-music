01 / System

Keep the identity raw, dark, physical, and legible.

Use SAP only as transition context. Erosyn is the current public name.

Core sentence

Erosyn blends experimental bass, grime pressure, DnB speed, psytrance tension, electro, hardstyle, and classic rave damage into a raw hybrid club sound.

  • ToneDirect, underground, physical, signal-heavy.
  • AvoidGlossy festival EDM, soft lifestyle framing, generic cyber copy.
  • ContextLate rooms, SoundCloud heads, warehouse systems, DJ bills.

Do

  • Use the flat logo for most production layouts.
  • Use red as signal, not wallpaper.
  • Keep text clean and readable around chaotic visuals.
  • Use the one-sheet for booking and press outreach.

Do not

  • Let SAP compete as the current name.
  • Use glow logos as the only logo source.
  • Trust generated SVG traces as final masters.
  • Build new assets around S-shaped legacy callbacks.
