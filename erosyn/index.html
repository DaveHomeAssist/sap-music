About

Raw hybrid club sound from the Miami underground.

NotesBio · Sound

Erosyn is Connor’s underground electronic producer/DJ project, based in Miami.

The music stays rooted in experimental bass, dubstep, DnB, UK grime, psytrance, electro, hardstyle, and classic rave — now sharpened into a darker visual system.

Corrupted low-end, grime pressure, rave damage, distorted club rhythms, red signal energy, and warehouse system intensity. Built for late rooms, heavy systems, and high-pressure hybrid bass sets.
